About ToxiDerma-XAI

Understanding arsenicosis and our AI solution

Arsenicosis is a chronic condition resulting from prolonged exposure to arsenic, commonly through contaminated groundwater. It manifests as distinctive dermatological lesions including hyperpigmentation, keratosis, and melanosis.

Our ToxiDerma-XAI system uses state-of-the-art deep learning to automatically detect these lesions, providing healthcare professionals with a powerful diagnostic assistance tool. The explainable AI component ensures transparency in decision-making.
