Advancing Medical Diagnosis Through AI
Arsenicosis is a chronic condition caused by prolonged exposure to arsenic, typically through contaminated drinking water. It manifests as distinctive skin lesions that can progress to serious complications if left untreated.
Our system uses a fine-tuned ResNet50 model trained on medical images. Upload an image, get instant classification with confidence scores, and view AI explanations showing which image regions influenced the decision.
